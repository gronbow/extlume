# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate
disruption are not accepted in project spaces. Technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation
when needed to protect contributors and users. Serious conduct concerns should
be reported privately to the maintainers rather than posted in a public issue.

By participating, contributors agree to follow these expectations in issues,
pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
